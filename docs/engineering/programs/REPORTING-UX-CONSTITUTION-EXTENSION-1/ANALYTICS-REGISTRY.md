| `sales-analytics` | Sales Analytics | What is operational sales activity and trend? | Sales Orders, Orders, trends, period detail | Sales | No |
| `financial-analytics` | Financial Analytics | What is financial / settlement performance in detail? | Total Sales detail, Net Sales, averages, tax | Financial | No |
| `refund-analytics` | Refund Analytics | How much was refunded and how? | Refund Amount, refund detail widgets | Financial (nested) | No |
| `payment-analytics` | Payment Analytics | How are customers paying? | Payment mix, tender totals | Financial (nested) | No |
