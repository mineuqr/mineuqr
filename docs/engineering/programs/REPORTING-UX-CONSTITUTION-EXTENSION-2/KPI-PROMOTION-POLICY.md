## Policy statement

Executive Dashboard is a **curated health summary**. Adding KPIs is exceptional. New metrics start outside Executive Overview and earn promotion through evidence.

## Hard rules

1. No KPI enters Executive Overview at Stage 1–5.  
2. Skipping promotion stages is prohibited.  
3. Executive KPI inflation is prohibited.  
4. Duplicates of an existing Executive business question are rejected (UX-03).  
5. Diagnostic KPIs (Class 4) are barred from Executive by default.  
6. Internal KPIs (Class 5) are permanently barred from customer Executive (and all customer reporting).  
7. Removing Executive KPIs is allowed when redundancy or low executive usefulness is proven.  
8. Non-KPI Executive widgets (e.g. Payment Overview) require the same protection gates for Executive placement.

## Evidence bar (must all pass)

| Requirement | Evidence type |
|-------------|---------------|
| Clear business value | Product brief / owner interview notes |
| No duplication | Business Question Registry check |
| Unique business question | UX-02 mapping |
| Stable calculation | Formula freeze + regression |
| Canonical ownership | KPI Ownership Registry |
| Canonical source | Source of Truth Registry |
| Consistent terminology | Product Semantics / KPI-04 |
| Successful UAT | UAT report |
| Executive usefulness | &lt; 10s health-summary fit review |

## Approvals required for Stage 6

- Promotion Review (complete)  
- Architecture Approval  
- Product Approval  
- Documentation Update (registries + dictionary class)  
- UAT  
- Production Certification  

## Demotion

Demotion from Class 1 / Executive removal requires Architecture + Product approval and registry updates; may retain the KPI in Financial / Operational / Diagnostic placement under its new class.
