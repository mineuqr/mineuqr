# Today Dashboard

## Question

**How is the restaurant doing today?**

## Scope

Current **Business Day** window from restaurant working hours (`businessDayTodayReportingBounds`).

## Cards (only)

Cash Sales · Card Sales · Refund Amount · Tax Collected · Orders · Net Sales

## Explicit exclusions

Average Order · Average Check · Average Ticket · charts · secondary ratios · payment method breakdown · catalog stats

## Success criterion

Owner understands the day within **five seconds**.
