# Animation Guidelines

## Allowed

| Motion | Duration | Purpose |
|--------|----------|---------|
| Hover translate / scale | ~200ms ease-out | Affordance |
| Shadow / glow intensify | ~200ms | Category recognition |
| Press scale | ~150–200ms | Click feedback |
| Ripple opacity | ~200ms | Press locus |

## Forbidden

- Continuous pulse / blink / flash loops  
- Infinite shimmer on KPI values  
- Staggered card entrance that delays comprehension  
- Parallax or decorative particle motion  

## Principle

**Elegant · Premium · Quiet.** Motion confirms interaction; stillness carries the numbers.
