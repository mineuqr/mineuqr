# Chart Priority Guidelines

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| **Program** | REPORTING-VISUAL-HIERARCHY-1 |
| **Date** | 2026-07-27 |

1. Charts **support** KPI cards — they do not replace them.  
2. Chart panels use supporting chrome (`chartSupporting`) and reduced height vs prior.  
3. Label charts as supporting where they reinforce (e.g. Refund trend).  
4. Trends remain under Sales Analytics; they must not dominate Executive.  
5. No decorative-only charts on Executive Overview.
